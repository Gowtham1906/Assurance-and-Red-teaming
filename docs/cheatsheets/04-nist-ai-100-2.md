## What it is

A NIST publication that gives a **taxonomy and terminology** for adversarial
machine learning. It is not a tool or a gate — it is a shared vocabulary so our
findings line up with US-government guidance.

## How it classifies attacks (per plan §3)

An attack is described along these axes:

| Axis | Question it answers | Example values |
|------|--------------------|-----------------|
| **System type** | What kind of AI? | Predictive ML, Generative AI |
| **Lifecycle stage** | When does the attack hit? | Training, deployment/inference |
| **Attacker goal** | What do they want? | Availability, integrity, privacy, misuse |
| **Attacker capability** | What can they do? | Control training data, query access, modify input |
| **Attacker knowledge** | What do they know? | White-box, black-box |
